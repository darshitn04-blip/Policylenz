# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY demo fact sheet

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) provides health coverage for eligible families. Eligibility is determined through official beneficiary data and applicable state implementation rules.

For this student demo, the checker only verifies that an Aadhaar document has been supplied. It cannot confirm a person's inclusion in the beneficiary database. Citizens should verify their status through an official PM-JAY channel and should not rely on this demonstration app for treatment or enrolment decisions.
