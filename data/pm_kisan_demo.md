# PM-KISAN demo fact sheet

PM-KISAN is an income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families. The benefit is Rs. 6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 directly to the beneficiary bank account.

For the simplified demo checker, a person should have cultivable land recorded in their name and should provide Aadhaar, an active bank account, and land-record information. Official eligibility and exclusions must always be verified on the PM-KISAN portal or with the local agriculture office.

The scheme documents and beneficiary records, not this application, determine final eligibility. This demo text is included for development only; replace it with the current official guideline PDF before public use.
